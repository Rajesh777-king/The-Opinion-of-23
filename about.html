Behind the journal

An Old Gen Z.

I'm Rajesh M Patil, 23—a brain-rotting Gen Z guy who sometimes thinks like a retired man waiting for his pension. Despite the brain rot, I'm serious about the world around me and have plenty of concerns about social life, society, and the way things work. With my modern-day computer science background, I thought, why not mix the social world with technology and use the internet to make my thoughts reach more people? Here, I share my opinions on articles I've read and topics that have caught my attention. This blog is also my personal litmus test: I want to see whether my thinking holds up, learn from criticism and appreciation, and discover whether I should stand by my views or change the direction of my thinking. So, read, question, criticise, or appreciate—I'm here not just to share my opinions, but to learn from yours too.
